# MeatCO: product-first redesign

Subject: an online butcher serving households in Tbilisi, with a separate restaurant supply path.
Main action: choose a raw meat product and prepare a WhatsApp order.

## Direction

- Claret #641e2c: brand, wordmark, primary actions.
- Forest #213b31: hero and restaurant supply.
- Porcelain #f7f8f4: main surface.
- Limestone #e8e9e1: product photography and soft panels.
- Ink #222b25: body copy.
- Muted sage #71796f: secondary labels.

Display: Prata (Latin/Cyrillic), Noto Serif Georgian (Georgian).
Body and controls: Manrope / Noto Sans Georgian already in the project.
Brand lettering remains MeatCO in the established heavy sans, with a small M seal.

Signature: a large MeatCO wordmark paired with a product tag naming the photographed cut and its actual price. Every product photograph has a restrained MeatCO corner label.
Avoid cooked food on the homepage, restaurant menu language, invented quality grades and generic stock photography of people or facilities.

## Structure

Utility strip with service area
Branded navigation
Raw meat hero + product tag
Compact category navigation
Eight distinct raw meat product cards with prices
MeatCO order and delivery process
Small optional grocery add-ons
Separate restaurant supply panel
Compact FAQ
Large brand footer

Full catalog: category filters, search, real product cards and progressive reveal.
Dish guide: retained as secondary help, linked from the catalog and footer.

## Critique before implementation

The previous giant photo and long text list made meat secondary while dish photography suggested a restaurant. A serif font alone would not solve this. The new structure devotes the main visual area to identifiable raw products with explicit units and prices; brand typography stays distinct from product typography.
Premium refers to presentation, not an unverified meat grade.
