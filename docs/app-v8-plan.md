# MeatCO v8: simpler ordering

Keep the existing static app, product data, multilingual search, basket storage and WhatsApp checkout. The owner requested an incremental UI refinement, not a new stack.

Palette: paper #F4EFE6, surface #FFFBF6, ink #1A1A17, forest #1C3A2C, wine #6E1F2A. Wine belongs only to the basket count and checkout. Prata / Noto Serif Georgian only for the home headline; Manrope / Noto Georgian for all controls, prices and headings.

Layout: one centered canvas, 480px on small screens and 1040px on desktop; home product grid becomes three columns on desktop. The signature is an uninterrupted meat photograph over a compact price label and an in-place weight control. No stamps, hearts, story tiles or decorative flame.

Home: headline -> one explanation -> factual trust chips -> four photo categories -> six selected products -> small brand line.

Catalog: sticky search and five chips -> six products initially -> more on request. Search keeps typo handling and reveals matching items. No sort, filter sheet or favorites.

Product sheet: large photo -> purpose -> quantity -> optional preparation note -> sticky add. Cart: lines -> estimate and exact-weight explanation -> address -> requested time chips -> comment -> optional photo -> WhatsApp. Preparation notes are transient and included in the existing message builder; private address and comments are not persisted.

Critique of the plan: beige alone cannot establish product character. Enlarge the photography, remove card padding around photos and use strong numerical price hierarchy; do not add more marketing panels. Events and restaurant supply stay under Help so the first purchase remains simple.

Factual limits: no confirmed reply SLA, same-day guarantee, vacuum packing, cold-pack details or minimum order. Do not invent these. Slots are customer preferences, explicitly confirmed in WhatsApp. No claim that the curated six products are proven bestsellers or in live stock.
